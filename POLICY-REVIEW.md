# Urban Shisha — policy content review

Prepared 8 October 2026. This is a content handoff, not a representation that the store is legally ready to launch.

The five policy pages are finished design previews with explicit draft status. They describe the current preview honestly and reserve unconfirmed business terms for finalisation. Do not silently remove draft notices while those details remain unknown.

| Page | Details to complete for the live store |
| --- | --- |
| Shipping | Serviceable locations/PIN codes, dispatch estimates, delivery estimates, fees, courier/tracking process, address corrections and handling of delivery failures |
| Returns | Return-request window, change-of-mind eligibility, category/condition rules, defective/wrong/damaged item process, return address, shipping costs, cancellation terms, refund methods and estimates |
| Privacy | Merchant/controller identity and contact, actual collected data/purposes, hosting/payment/messaging providers, cookies/analytics, retention, applicable request/complaint procedures and live-account handling |
| Terms | Legal merchant name, address, registration details, support/grievance contact, live product/pricing/tax/payment information, order-acceptance process and final purchase terms |
| 18+ | Actual order-age verification process, any information collected, delivery checks and applicable product/location restrictions |

The live policies must match the final WooCommerce configuration and operational practices. The preview deliberately does not invent free delivery, a return window, refund guarantees, stock, checkout acceptance, fixed response times or universal product legality. Draft wording does not exclude applicable statutory rights.

Privacy facts reflect the current code: local saved bag/wishlist/age/bulk selections; no store-backend submission from preview forms; prepared wholesale messages are shared only through customer-selected external channels. Recheck every factual statement after adding hosting, analytics, payments, WordPress plugins or server-side forms. Browser autofill is managed by the browser and is not removed by our saved-data control.
