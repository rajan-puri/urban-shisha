OUR POINT OF VIEW.

IN THE DETAILS.

A setup is a collection of choices. We give each one room to stand out.

Urban Shisha brings hookahs and their accessories into one place. From a distinctive glass base to the bowl, hose and tools that complete it, the focus is on the pieces and how they come together.

Whether you’re exploring your first setup, choosing a replacement part or putting together an order for your business, start with the collection. If you need help with fit or product details, talk to us before you buy.
